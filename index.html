💙 About the Project

Technology With a Purpose

Marine pollution requires practical solutions as well as reliable information. Ocean × Eco-Tech was created as a student innovation project to explore how a physical waste collection prototype can be supported by a transparent digital platform.

Rather than presenting unverified numbers as facts, the system is designed to distinguish clearly between project goals, proposed capabilities, user-created records and measurements generated by real hardware.

✨ Note from the Team

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a student-developed prototype created to explore practical approaches to marine waste management. Under controlled testing conditions, the prototype has demonstrated approximately 90% accuracy, while continued development, independent validation and real-world testing remain essential before broader deployment.
