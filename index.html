🌊 STUDENT-DRIVEN OCEAN INNOVATION

Technology for a Cleaner Ocean.

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a student-developed environmental technology project exploring how smart systems can help detect, collect, monitor and understand marine waste.

🌱 OUR MISSION

Turning an environmental challenge into an opportunity for innovation.

Marine pollution affects ecosystems, wildlife and coastal environments around the world. Ocean × Eco-Tech explores a technology-based approach to identifying and collecting floating waste while keeping environmental safety at the centre of the design.
