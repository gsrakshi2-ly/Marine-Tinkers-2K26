Technology With a Purpose

Ocean pollution is not only an environmental challenge. It is also an opportunity to explore how technology can help communities understand and address problems around them.

Our project combines a physical marine waste collector with a digital interface designed for future monitoring and analysis.

  • 🌊 Reduce floating waste
  • ♻️ Encourage responsible waste management
  • 🐠 Consider marine-life safety
  • 💡 Encourage student innovation
🌍

🎯 Our Environmental Goals

The areas where the project hopes to create value.

🧴

Reduce Plastic Pollution

Explore ways to intercept floating waste before it travels further through waterways.

🐟

Protect Marine Life

Include safety considerations when designing technology that operates around aquatic life.

📚

Educate & Inspire

Show how environmental science, electronics and coding can work together in a student project.

🤖

Explore Automation

Investigate how future sensors and microcontrollers could make collection smarter.

📱

Connect Physical & Digital

Create a digital interface that can eventually display information from the physical prototype.

🌱

Encourage Sustainability

Promote practical environmental awareness through technology and community participation.
