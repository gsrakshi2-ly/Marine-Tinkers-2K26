Our Impact

Ocean × Eco-Tech is designed to explore how technology, responsible innovation and student creativity can contribute to cleaner and safer oceans.

🌍 Why This Project Matters

Marine waste affects ecosystems, wildlife and coastal environments. Our project explores a practical technology-based approach to addressing this challenge.

♻️

Cleaner Waters

The proposed collector is designed to help identify and collect floating waste from aquatic environments, supporting cleaner waterways.

🐠

Marine-Life Safety

The system concept includes safety-focused sensing intended to help reduce the risk of operating near marine life.

🌱

Sustainable Thinking

The project encourages the use of technology to address environmental challenges while considering responsible and sustainable design.

🌊 From Idea to Environmental Innovation

The project combines environmental awareness, engineering concepts and software development into one student-built system.

💡 The Vision

Ocean × Eco-Tech began with a simple question: how can technology help us respond to the growing problem of marine waste?

The answer we are exploring is a smart floating waste-collection system that can eventually connect physical hardware with a digital monitoring platform.

Instead of treating the prototype as only a mechanical device, the project brings together hardware, sensors, software, mapping and environmental thinking.

🌱 Our goal is to turn a student idea into a meaningful environmental technology concept.
