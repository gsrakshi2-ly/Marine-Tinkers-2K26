🌊 Where We Can Make a Difference

The project focuses on several environmental areas.

♻️

Waste Reduction

A smart collection system can help demonstrate how floating waste could be removed from waterways.

🐠

Marine Protection

Future safety systems can be designed to reduce interaction between the collector and marine life.

📡

Environmental Monitoring

Sensors can eventually provide useful information about water conditions and environmental changes.

🌱

Sustainability

The project encourages responsible technology and environmental thinking.

📚

Education

Students can learn how coding, electronics and environmental science can work together.

🤝

Community Action

Technology can support awareness and encourage communities to make sustainable choices.
