🌍 ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Working toward cleaner oceans

Ocean × Eco-Tech aims to explore practical approaches for reducing floating marine waste and encouraging responsible environmental action. The project is designed as a prototype rather than a claim of large-scale environmental impact.

🌊

Cleaner Waterways

The prototype explores how floating waste collection technology could support cleaner aquatic environments.

🐠

Marine-Life Protection

Reducing the presence of waste in aquatic environments can support the broader goal of protecting marine ecosystems.

📚

Environmental Awareness

The project encourages students and communities to observe, document and think critically about marine waste.
