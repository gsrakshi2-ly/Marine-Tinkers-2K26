⚙️ Ocean × Eco-Tech

Technology Behind the Mission

Ocean × Eco-Tech combines a physical marine-waste collection concept with a digital monitoring platform. The system is designed to collect, process and document information responsibly while keeping sensor-dependent functions unavailable until real hardware data exists.

🌊 Technology Overview

One System, Multiple Technologies

The project brings together mechanical design, electronics, sensing, communication and web development into one student-built environmental technology concept.

🚤

Physical Collection Technology

The floating prototype is designed around a collection mechanism capable of directing floating waste into a designated storage area. Propulsion and structural components are intended to support controlled movement while maintaining stability on the water.

💻

Digital Monitoring Technology

The web application provides interfaces for live hardware data, geographic visualization, waste reporting, collection history, project progress and future analytics.

✨ Note from the Team

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a student-developed prototype created to explore practical approaches to marine waste management. Under controlled testing conditions, the prototype has demonstrated approximately 90% accuracy, while continued development, independent validation and real-world testing remain essential before broader deployment.
