About Our Mission

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a student-driven project exploring how technology, environmental awareness and creative engineering can come together to tackle aquatic waste.

Technology for a Cleaner Ocean

Our project focuses on creating a marine waste collection concept that combines a physical prototype with a digital application.

The goal is to explore how engineering, electronics and software can work together to address an environmental challenge.

  • 🌊 Ocean-focused innovation
  • ♻️ Responsible waste management
  • 🤖 Eco-tech and automation
  • 🐠 Marine-life awareness

🔧 What We're Building

The project combines several areas of technology.

🚤

Floating Prototype

A prototype designed to move across water and collect floating waste.

⚙️

Electronics

A future hardware layer can use a microcontroller and sensors.

💻

Web Application

A digital interface designed to display information and interact with the project.

🗺️

Mapping

Future location information can help visualize collection and monitoring areas.

📊

Data Analysis

Sensor data can eventually be transformed into useful environmental information.

Safety Systems
