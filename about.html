About Ocean × Eco-Tech

A student-led eco-tech concept combining environmental awareness, smart technology and marine protection to tackle ocean waste.

Technology for a Cleaner Ocean

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a prototype concept designed to explore how technology can help address floating waste in waterways.

The project combines a marine waste-collection system with future sensor technology, environmental monitoring and marine-life protection features.

Our goal is not just to collect waste, but to understand how technology, sustainability and responsible human action can work together.
