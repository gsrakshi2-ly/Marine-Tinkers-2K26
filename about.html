ℹ️ ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Ocean × Eco-Tech

A student-developed environmental technology project exploring practical ways to address marine waste through engineering, electronics and digital tools.

🌊 OUR STORY

Where technology meets ocean responsibility

Ocean × Eco-Tech was developed as a student innovation project with the goal of exploring how a combination of physical collection technology and digital monitoring could contribute to more responsible marine-waste management.

The project brings together prototype engineering, sensor integration, mapping and web development into one connected concept. The platform is designed to display information honestly, with hardware-dependent features remaining unavailable until genuine data becomes available.

👩‍🎓 STUDENT-BUILT

Built as a student innovation

This project is independently developed by students as an exploration of environmental engineering and technology. Its purpose is to learn, experiment, prototype and demonstrate how digital systems can support environmental awareness.
