ℹ️ About the Project

Technology for a Cleaner Ocean

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a student-developed environmental technology project exploring how a floating waste collection prototype and a digital monitoring platform can work together to support cleaner aquatic environments.

🌊 Our Project

From an Idea to a Working Concept

The project combines engineering, environmental awareness and web development into one student-led prototype.

🌍 What is Ocean × Eco-Tech?

Ocean × Eco-Tech is designed around the idea of using accessible technology to support marine-waste collection and environmental monitoring.

The physical prototype is intended to collect floating waste while future sensor integration can provide verified information about the prototype's surroundings and operation.

Alongside the prototype, the web application provides areas for live hardware data, mapping, waste documentation, project history and environmental impact.

🐠 Our Vision

We envision a practical system that connects responsible waste collection with transparent environmental data, helping students and communities understand how technology can contribute to ocean conservation.
