About Ocean × Eco-Tech

A student-focused marine eco-tech concept combining environmental awareness, smart technology and sustainable innovation to imagine cleaner waterways.

🌍 Our Mission

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a concept project designed to explore how technology can help address marine pollution.

The idea combines a physical waste collection prototype with a digital platform for environmental monitoring, mapping and awareness.

The current website can demonstrate the concept even before real sensors are connected.
