About Ocean × Eco-Tech

A student-led concept combining ocean conservation, electronics, programming and sustainable technology to explore new ways of tackling aquatic waste.

Technology With a Purpose

Ocean × Eco-Tech is a prototype concept designed around one simple idea: technology should help us understand and protect the environment.

The project combines a web application, environmental monitoring concepts and a future physical waste-collection system.

At the current stage, the website works as a software prototype. Physical sensors can be integrated later as the hardware develops.

🐠

🌱 Our Mission

Four ideas guide the Ocean × Eco-Tech project.

🌊

Protect Water

Explore technological approaches that could help reduce pollution in aquatic environments.

🤖

Build Smart Systems

Combine programming, electronics and automation into useful environmental technology.

🐠

Protect Marine Life

Consider marine-life safety when designing future autonomous collection systems.

⚙️ How the Concept Works

The project is designed in layers.

01

Detect

Future sensors can detect environmental conditions and objects around the system.

02

Analyse

Data can be processed by the control system and displayed digitally.

03

Respond

The future hardware could make decisions based on sensor information.

04

Learn

Environmental information could help improve future versions of the system.
