Our Marine Waste Collector

The proposed prototype is a floating eco-tech system designed to collect surface waste while leaving room for future smart monitoring and safety features.

  • 🌊 Floating structure
  • ♻️ Waste collection system
  • ⚙️ Motorized movement
  • 📡 Future sensor integration
  • 🐠 Marine-life safety concept
  • 📱 Future app connectivity

⚙️ How the Concept Works

The basic workflow of the planned prototype.

01

Move

The floating platform moves through the selected water area.

02

Collect

Surface waste is directed toward the collection mechanism.

03

Sort

Future sensors can help identify different categories of collected material.

04

Monitor

Future electronics can send information to the connected application.
