🔄 How the Concept Works

A simple four-stage approach.

01

Detect

Identify floating waste and environmental conditions.

02

Collect

Move collected waste into the prototype's collection area.

03

Monitor

Observe environmental information through connected systems.

04

Improve

Use observations to improve future versions of the system.
