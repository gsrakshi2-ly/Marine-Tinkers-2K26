♻️ Solutions

Turning Ocean Waste Into Action

Ocean × Eco-Tech combines waste collection, responsible reporting and future sensor integration into a structured approach for documenting and addressing marine waste.

🌊 Proposed Solutions

A Practical Approach to Marine Waste

The project focuses on practical steps that can support waste collection and environmental documentation without presenting proposed capabilities as completed results.

♻️

Floating Waste Collection

The prototype is designed to collect suitable floating waste from the water surface and organize collected material for documentation.

📡

Sensor-Based Monitoring

Future hardware integration can provide verified measurements such as distance or environmental observations when sensors are connected and tested.

📸

Waste Reporting

Users can document observed waste through the Get Involved page, creating a structured record of environmental observations.

🗺️

Location Mapping

Verified monitoring locations can be displayed on the world map once reliable location data becomes available.

📚

Collection History

Actual waste reports and collection records can be retained as a history for reviewing project activity over time.

🌱

Responsible Awareness

The platform helps communicate the importance of reducing marine waste and protecting aquatic ecosystems.
