Technology Meets Nature

Ocean pollution is a major environmental challenge. Plastic waste, changing water conditions and damage to marine ecosystems can affect the health of our oceans.

Ocean × Eco-Tech proposes a technology-based approach where sensors, data analysis and smart waste collection work together to monitor problems and support solutions.

The goal is simple: use innovation responsibly to help people understand ocean health and encourage action that protects marine environments.
