Built for a Sustainable Future

Ocean × Eco-Tech explores how engineering, environmental awareness and responsible technology can work together to support cleaner and healthier water environments.

🌍 Technology With Environmental Purpose

Sustainability is more than collecting waste. It also means thinking about how technology is designed, used, maintained and improved over time.

Our project uses the idea of an intelligent waste collection system as a starting point for exploring practical environmental technology.

🌍

🧠 Responsible Technology

Good environmental technology also needs careful design.

✅ What We Aim For

  • 🌱 Minimise unnecessary environmental impact.
  • ⚡ Use energy efficiently wherever possible.
  • ♻️ Encourage responsible handling of collected waste.
  • 🔧 Design systems that can be maintained and improved.
  • 📊 Use data to make informed design decisions.

🔮 What Must Be Considered

  • 🔋 Energy consumption during operation.
  • 🐟 Possible effects on aquatic organisms.
  • 🌊 Performance under different water conditions.
  • 🛠️ Maintenance and durability of components.
  • ♻️ Proper disposal or recycling of collected material.
