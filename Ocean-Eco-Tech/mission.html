Our Mission

Our mission is to develop and demonstrate a smart, environmentally conscious approach to floating waste management. Waste Collector 2K26 combines sensors, automation and sustainable thinking to explore how technology can help keep water bodies cleaner.

🔭

Our Vision

We envision a future where environmental monitoring and smart technology work together to identify problems early, support responsible waste management and encourage communities to protect aquatic ecosystems.
