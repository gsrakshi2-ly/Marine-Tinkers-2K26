Our Project Journey

From identifying a real environmental problem to developing a smart technology concept — follow the journey of Waste Collector 2K26.

🌱 From Idea to Innovation

Every project begins with a problem. Our journey started with the challenge of floating waste in water bodies and developed into an exploration of sensors, automation, data and sustainable engineering.

💡
STAGE 01

Identifying the Problem

We identified floating waste as an environmental challenge that can affect waterways and aquatic ecosystems. The first step was understanding why a smarter solution could be useful.

🔍
STAGE 02

Research & Exploration

We explored existing waste-management approaches and studied how sensors, automation and environmental technology could be combined into one concept.

📝
STAGE 03

Designing the Solution

The Waste Collector 2K26 concept was developed with the aim of detecting and collecting floating waste while keeping environmental impact in mind.

🛠️
STAGE 04

Building the Prototype

The physical prototype helped us visualise how the collection mechanism, sensors and control system could work together.

📡
STAGE 05

Sensor Integration

Sensors were introduced to demonstrate how the system could detect objects and collect useful environmental information.

🧠
STAGE 06

Smart Analysis

The website interface was designed to demonstrate how sensor information could be transformed into dashboards, analytics, maps and environmental insights.

🚀
STAGE 07

Future Development

Future versions could explore IoT connectivity, AI-assisted waste identification, improved energy management and larger-scale testing.

🏆 Where We Are Now

Waste Collector 2K26 has evolved from an environmental problem into a technology-based prototype concept. The project combines hardware, software, sustainability and teamwork to demonstrate a possible approach to waterway waste management.

🔮 What's Next?

The journey doesn't stop with the prototype. These are some areas that could make the system more capable in future development.

📶

IoT Connectivity

Connect the system to a remote monitoring platform.

🤖

AI Recognition

Explore automated classification of different waste types.

🔋

Energy Optimisation

Improve power efficiency for longer operating periods.

🌊 Innovation Is a Journey

The goal is not simply to build a machine. It is to explore how technology and environmental responsibility can work together.
