How It Works

From detecting a problem in the water to collecting waste and displaying useful information, discover the complete working concept behind Ocean × Eco-Tech.

⚙️ The Complete Process

A simple step-by-step view of how the system can operate.

01
🌊

Monitor the Water

The system is placed in a selected water body or test environment. Sensors and observation systems can monitor relevant environmental conditions.

02
📡

Detect

A sensor detects a nearby object or a change in the monitored condition and produces a corresponding electrical signal.

03
🧠

Process the Signal

The controller receives the signal and processes it according to the programmed logic of the prototype.

04
⚙️

Activate the System

If the required condition is detected, the controller can activate a connected mechanism such as a motor or collection system.

05
♻️

Collect Waste

The collection mechanism guides suitable floating material toward a designated collection area where it can later be removed.

06
📊

Record & Display

Relevant information can be recorded and displayed on the digital platform to help users understand what is happening in the monitored area.

📡 Digital Monitoring Layer

The physical system can be connected to a digital monitoring platform.
