Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ocean × Eco-Tech, the Waste Collector 2K26 concept, its technology, purpose and future possibilities.

Click a question to reveal its answer.

Ocean × Eco-Tech is an environmental innovation project that combines marine awareness with technology to explore solutions for floating waste in water environments.

Waste Collector 2K26 is the project's prototype concept. It explores how a floating system could help collect suitable waste from the surface of water.

Floating waste can affect aquatic ecosystems, water quality and the appearance and usability of water bodies. Reducing waste at its source is therefore an important environmental goal.

Sensors can provide information about objects or environmental conditions. A controller can then process those inputs and use programmed logic to determine an appropriate response.

The current website interface uses demonstration data unless a real sensor system and backend are connected. The interface is designed so real-time data can be integrated later.

Yes. A future version could explore AI-assisted identification of different types of floating objects, anomaly detection and smarter environmental data analysis.

The concept can support automation by combining sensors, a controller and programmed logic. The exact level of automation depends on the hardware and software used.

The main goal is to demonstrate how innovation, technology and environmental responsibility can work together to address a real-world problem.

Future improvements could include better sensors, IoT connectivity, AI analysis, improved energy management, remote monitoring and a stronger collection mechanism.

The current project is a prototype and educational innovation concept. Large-scale deployment would require extensive engineering, environmental testing and safety evaluation.
