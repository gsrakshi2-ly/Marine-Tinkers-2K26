🌱 Project Overview

Ocean × Eco-Tech is an environmental innovation project that explores how sensors, electronics, mechanical systems and digital tools can be combined to address floating waste in water environments.

The Waste Collector 2K26 concept focuses on detecting, collecting and monitoring suitable floating waste while promoting awareness of responsible environmental technology.

🧪 Testing & Observations

Testing helps identify what works and what can be improved.

✅ What We Check

  • 📡 Whether sensors respond to intended inputs.
  • ⚙️ Whether the mechanical mechanism operates correctly.
  • 🔋 Whether the power system supports the components.
  • 🧠 Whether programmed logic produces the expected response.
  • 🌊 Whether the prototype remains suitable for the intended environment.

🔧 Areas for Improvement

  • ⚡ Improve energy efficiency.
  • 📡 Increase detection reliability.
  • ⚙️ Improve collection mechanism performance.
  • 🛠️ Strengthen the physical structure.
  • 📊 Improve monitoring and data visualisation.
