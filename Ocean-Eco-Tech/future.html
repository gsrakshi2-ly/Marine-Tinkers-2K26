🗺️ Project Roadmap

From a student innovation concept toward a scalable environmental technology system.

PHASE 01

🌱 Build the Foundation

Develop the website, prototype the concept, research environmental challenges and create the initial monitoring model.

PHASE 02

📡 Connect Real Sensors

Introduce physical sensors that can collect environmental measurements and send them to a central platform.

PHASE 03

🤖 Add Intelligent Analysis

Use machine-learning techniques to analyze historical readings and identify unusual patterns.

PHASE 04

🛸 Develop Smart Collection

Explore autonomous systems capable of assisting with floating-waste collection in monitored areas.

PHASE 05

🌍 Scale the Network

Connect multiple monitoring locations and create a broader environmental intelligence platform.

PHASE 06

🌊 Global Ocean Protection

Use technology, collaboration and environmental data to support marine conservation at a much larger scale.
